Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Because we are equipped with a 45x stereo microscope and micro-soldering rework stations, ripped or lifted pads are a standard repair for us. We bridge severed traces using microscopic 0.02mm enamelled pure copper jumper wires and seal them permanently with UV-cured solder mask.
Every HDMI port replacement and thermal servicing is warrantied for 90 days. If the replaced port fails or video cuts out under normal gaming use, we repair or replace it free of charge. (Physical impact or dropped console damage is excluded).
The PS5 uses liquid metal (gallium-indium alloy) rather than standard thermal paste. Over time, dry oxidation spots form on the APU surface, causing hot spots and sudden shut-downs. We safely decontaminate the oxidized layer, inspect the foam barrier seal, and respread fresh ultra-pure liquid metal.
When you submit a ticket selecting "Nationwide Mail-In", you'll receive a printable packing slip and packing guide. Ship just your console unit (no cables or controllers needed). Once it arrives, we inspect it, complete the repair with a fast turnaround, and mail it back with tracking and insurance.
